Vibecheck is a private, local-first mood and symptom tracker designed for complete privacy, ease of use, and low friction.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>1-Tap Quick Logging: Log your mood in seconds with minimal friction</li>
<li>Symptom & Energy Tracking: Track focus, energy, sensory levels, and sleep</li>
<li>Dopamine Menu: Grounding exercises and gentle prompts for self-regulation</li>
<li>Calendar Heatmap & Insights: Spot patterns and understand your mental health over time</li>
<li>Custom Moods & Tags: Personalize your tracking experience</li>
<li>JSON Data Export & Import: Complete ownership of your data</li>
<li>100% Offline: Operates completely on-device without internet access</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No data ever leaves your device. No cloud sync, no user accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party tracking SDKs.
